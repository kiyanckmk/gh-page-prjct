What a Game Developer Actually Does

Making games is far more than writing code. As a solo developer I wear many hats:

💡 Game Design

Coming up with core mechanics, balancing difficulty and economy, and making sure every minute of play feels rewarding.

👨‍💻 Programming

Writing gameplay systems, UI, save data, physics and tools. Optimizing so games run smoothly on low-end devices.

🎨 Art & Animation

Creating sprites, 3D models, UI and effects, or working with artists to keep a consistent visual style.

🔊 Audio

Choosing music and sound effects that give every jump, click and win its own satisfying feedback.

🧪 Testing & QA

Playtesting with friends and the community, fixing bugs and polishing rough edges before each release.

📣 Marketing & Community

Posting devlogs, building a wishlist, answering player feedback and shipping updates people ask for.
